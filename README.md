## Arsen Amantay

Founder and CEO at **[NEVOD](https://nevod.kz)**. Based in Astana, Kazakhstan.

We build **autonomous defense drones and the modules that make them autonomous**. The aircraft finds the target, decides, and launches on its own. An operator-confirmation mode ships alongside it, because on several markets a human in the loop is a condition of entry rather than a preference.

Piloted FPV is no longer the innovation, it is the norm, and its ceiling is known: one drone costs one trained human, one radio link, one person's reaction time and fatigue. The next step is taking the human out of that loop. We start with defense against FPV kamikaze drones, because it is the easier half to prove and nobody argues about whether it should exist.

Before this I practiced law and built companies: Sterling law firm, Mango Travel, edupro.kz.
